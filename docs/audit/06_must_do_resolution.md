## 3. Results that weaken earlier claims (reported in the paper)

- **Over-detection.** The A1 models raised the over-detection rate on MNIST and PneumoniaMNIST. MNIST patch black deletion went from 0.048 to 0.125, and PneumoniaMNIST patch black deletion from 0.56 to 0.68 (Appendix C, Table 13).
- **PneumoniaMNIST patch AUROC.** The per-image AUROC on PneumoniaMNIST patch is not above 0.5 for black deletion: 0.46 [0.39, 0.54], Holm p = 1.000. Above r_c = 60% it falls to 0.00–0.08.
- **Wrong-way trends on PneumoniaMNIST texture.** Black deletion moves the wrong way in the form-matched comparisons (ρ = −0.48 vs random, −0.58 vs placebo), and barely responds against the size-matched map (ρ = 0.02, p_bh = 0.891).
- **Pet near-ties.** On Pet the low-reliance AUC gaps are near ties (median −0.060 to 0.000), so the early Pet switch points must be read with that caution.
