## Metric responsiveness to reliance (oracle maps)

responsiveness_rho: Spearman correlation between reliance and preference for the shortcut map (ideal: close to 1). decision_accuracy: share of models where the preferred map matches whether reliance is above 50%. switch_point_reliance: reliance where preference crosses 0.5 (ideal: 50). comparison: which two oracle maps are compared (preference = share of images where the metric rates the first above the second). Size-matched object: a binary map of the object's brightest pixels, as many as the shortcut. Placebo: the shortcut's shape at a wrong class's position, which never carries evidence. Random: independent uniform scores per pixel (a fully random ordering). The placebo's non-patch pixels are tied and ordered randomly, so it behaves almost like the random map ('placebo vs random' stays near 0.5). 'Placebo vs full object' and 'random vs full object' should be LOW at low reliance; a rise at high reliance is correct, because the object map then explains the model worse than a random ordering. switch_ci_low/high and rho_ci_low/high: 95% bootstrap intervals (2000 resamples of the seeds within each dose). switch_found_share: share of resamples in which preference crosses 0.5 at all (the switch interval is left empty below 0.5).
